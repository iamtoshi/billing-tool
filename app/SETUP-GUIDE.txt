DEO RATAN BILLING - SETUP GUIDE
================================

A. PUT IT ONLINE (once, by Toshi)
1. Cloudflare dashboard > Workers & Pages > Create > Pages > Upload assets.
2. Give it a name (e.g. deoratan-billing) and drag in this zip (or the unzipped folder).
3. Deploy. You get an address like https://deoratan-billing.pages.dev

B. SET UP THE OFFICE LAPTOP (once, needs internet)
1. Install Google Drive for desktop and sign in with the business Google account.
   It appears as "Google Drive" in File Explorer.
2. In File Explorer: Google Drive > My Drive > create a folder "Deo Ratan Billing".
   Right-click it > Offline access > Available offline.
3. Open Google Chrome and go to the app's address.
4. Click "Install app" (top right of the app, or the install icon in Chrome's address bar).
   The app now has its own icon on the desktop / Start menu.
5. In the app: Choose folder > pick Google Drive > My Drive > Deo Ratan Billing > Select Folder.
   When Chrome asks, click "Edit files". If it offers "Allow on every visit", pick that.

C. EVERY DAY
- Open "Deo Ratan Billing" from the Start menu. Works with or without internet.
- Bills save automatically into the Deo Ratan Billing folder; Google Drive uploads them when online.
- In that folder:  bills-data.json (the bills - don't edit or delete)
                   Aata sale statement.xlsx (Aata bills, including chapad)
                   Grain sale statement.xlsx (a tab for each grain: Wheat, Tewda, Chana, Soybean)
                   Grain purchase statement.xlsx (a tab for each grain, plus Dhan)
                   Master statement.xlsx (everything above in one file, as tabs)
                   All four update after every change - open them any time. The Aata page downloads the
                   Aata statement; the Grains page downloads the grain sale and purchase statements; both
                   pages can download the master statement.
                   Invoices (a PDF of every invoice and purchase slip)
                   Backups (one copy per day, last 30 days)
- Download bill (PDF) puts 3 copies on one A4 page in the Downloads folder: cut on the dashed lines;
  each copy has a blank strip on its left for punching.
- A single-copy PDF of every invoice is also saved in the Invoices folder.
- One bill can have up to 8 items. Bag sizes LD, 25.1 kg and 30 kg fill in the weight by themselves;
  choose Other to type the weight of one bag in kg.
- Aata or Chapad: each item on an Aata bill starts with "Item" - choose Aata (आटा) or Chapad (चापड़).
  That is the description printed on the bill. Sell either or both: use "+ Add another item" for more lines.
- The customer's mobile number prints on the bill, next to the GST No.
- The party's Location prints after their name and address on every bill and slip (not repeated if
  the address already has it).
- Saved parties: while making any bill, a "Saved parties" box on the right lists customers and sellers
  you've dealt with. Type part of a name or mobile number and click one to fill in mobile, name,
  location and GST. Typing a saved party's full name or mobile number also fills in the rest.
- Discount (Rs): one amount per bill, under the goods. It comes off before GST (sales) or off the
  purchase value (purchases), and prints on the bill only when used.
- Book no.: in the Aata and Grains lists, click "+ Add" in the Book no. column (or the Book no. on a
  bill's page) to write the account book number for that bill. Change or clear it any time.
  The customer's mobile number shows under their name in the lists.
- Made a bill by mistake? Open it > "Delete this bill". A backup copy is saved first. If it was the
  latest bill, its number is used again for the next one.
- Three tabs: Aata (wheat flour bills, numbered 01, 02...), Grains, Parties.
- Grains tab > New bill: pick the grain (Wheat, Tewda, Chana, Soybean, Dhan), then Sale or Purchase.
  Dhan is purchase only.
  * Sale (numbered GS-01, GS-02...): same as an Aata bill. Wheat is entered as no. of bags (30 kg, or Other
    to type the bag weight), like Aata; Tewda, Chana and Soybean as quantity in quintal. Wheat also needs its type (1544, Lokwan or Sharbati grades, Jhiri, Malwa, Shri Ram, Lota, or
    Others). A wheat line with type "Others" is sold loose like the other grains: quantity in quintal and rate
    only (no bags, size or brand; the name is optional). Bagged wheat lines can also have a brand, listed
    A to Z (Others to type one). GST is optional.
  * Purchase (numbered GP-01, GP-02...): enter vehicle, gross weight (quintal) and rate. Wheat types
    include Mill Quality and Others (type the name). The app takes off
    300 g per quintal, works out net weight x rate, then takes off dharam kata (Truck Rs 100; every other
    vehicle Rs 60) and labour per quintal of net weight, rounded (.5 and above goes up): Hydraulic trolley Rs 7.5;
    Truck, Tractor, Pickup and Others Rs 10.5. This applies to every slip.
    Dhan: 1 kg per quintal is taken off (not 300 g) and labour is Rs 15 per quintal, whatever the vehicle.
    What's left is payable to the seller, printed on a Purchase Slip.
- Parties tab: one ledger per party (customers and sellers). Debit = bills to them and payments we make;
  Credit = payments we receive and purchases from them. "we owe" means we still have to pay them.
  Add old dues and record payments there; the PDF button opens that invoice or slip.

D. IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG
- "Open my bills" screen: click it and choose Allow. Normal after Chrome restarts.
- "Can't find your bills file": make sure Google Drive is running (icon near the clock), then Try again.
- Asked to choose the folder again (e.g. after clearing Chrome data): pick the SAME
  Deo Ratan Billing folder - all bills come back.
- Red "Not saved" in the top bar: click it and follow the steps. Nothing is lost.
- Use the app in ONE window, on ONE laptop. Don't open the same folder from two computers.

E. SHIPPING A NEW VERSION (for whoever updates the app)
- Change the VERSION line at the top of sw.js, then upload the files to Cloudflare again.
- Open apps show "A new version of the app is ready - Update now". Bills are not affected.
